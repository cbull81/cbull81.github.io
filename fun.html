Browser Tracking Demo

A fake clickbait blog that shows you exactly what data websites tend to collect about you in real time — screen resolution, timezone, mouse movement, scroll depth, typing cadence, and more. All using standard browser APIs, no special permissions required. Nothing leaves your browser.

Wikipedia Knowledge Graphs

A comprehensive knowledge graph of the Wikipedia Math and Philosophy Portals, generated by evaluating the link structure of all relevant pages. The goal is to evaluate how concepts are interlinked on Wikipedia, and how different frameworks can coincide or diverge. Searching for a specific topic allows the user to view all connected topics and their functional relationships to each other. Inspired by my attempt to understand the measure-theoretic foundations of probability in my coursework. Also included is a 'create-your-own' knowledge graph tool, where you may enter any collection of Wikipedia pages and attempt to recover missing links between them.

Tacticos and Galacticos

An arcade-style draft soccer game where you pick players from randomly drawn teams across the last ten seasons of Europe's top five soccer leagues. Based on the advanced performance statistics of each player, a predicition algorithm estimates your team's performance over a 38-game season, and assigns your final league position and points total. Alternatively, switch over to World Cup 2026 mode, where you can draft a team from the 48 qualified squads for the 2026 tournament and try to win it all. Inspired by my love of both soccer and data science, modeled after 82-0.com, the NBA analogue to this game.
